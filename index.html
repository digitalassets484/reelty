FAQ

The honest questions

Why not just pay HeyGen $29 myself?

Because HeyGen hands you an empty editor. Reelty hands you a finished post. We write the real-estate script, bake in your brokerage disclosure, design the matching graphic, and schedule it to every platform. Listing in, post out — not a blank timeline.

Do I need to film myself?

Once. On Growth and Pro we create a digital twin from a short training clip during setup, then every future video uses it — no camera required. Starter uses a photo avatar.

What’s the setup fee for?

A one-time charge that covers building your avatar, spinning up your scheduling account, connecting your socials and loading your brand kit. It’s waived when you pay annually.

Will it post things without me seeing them?

No. ‘Approve before posting’ is on by default. You review every video and caption before it goes live. You can turn it off once you trust it.
